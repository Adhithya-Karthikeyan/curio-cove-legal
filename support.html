🏝️ What Curio Cove is

Curio Cove is a warm, safe learning app for curious kids (target ages 8–10). Your child asks friendly characters anything they wonder about and gets short, kind, age-appropriate answers. Every answer is made privately on your device — there is no internet, no accounts for your child, no ads, and no tracking.

📱 Device requirements

Curio Cove runs on:

  • iOS 26 / iPadOS 26 (or later).
  • Apple Intelligence-capable devices:
    • iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 or later model
    • iPad with A17 Pro, M1, or later

Curio Cove is free to download. The first 3 friends are free forever with no limits. A one-time in-app purchase of Unlock All Friends ($9.99 USD) unlocks all 10 friends. Family Sharing is supported — one purchase covers the whole family.

If your device doesn't yet have Apple Intelligence turned on, the app shows a friendly "needs Apple Intelligence" screen instead of failing. To enable it, open Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri on a supported device and follow the prompts. Both the device and Siri language must be one that Apple Intelligence supports. Then reopen Curio Cove.

🔓 Unlocking all friends

The first 3 friendly characters are always free, with no limits or time restrictions. To unlock all 10 friends, a grown-up can make a one-time in-app purchase of Unlock All Friends ($9.99 USD, or your region's equivalent price).

How purchasing works

The purchase screen is in the grown-up area, protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Children see a gentle "Ask a grown-up" card with no price or buy button. Only a grown-up can make purchases.

Family Sharing

Family Sharing is on. When a grown-up in your family group buys Unlock All Friends, all family members get it on their devices.

Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or use it on another device, you can restore your purchases from inside the grown-up area by tapping Restore Purchases. This talks to Apple's servers only — we never see your payment details or Apple Account information.

Ask to Buy

If Ask to Buy is set up in your Family Group, children's purchase requests will appear for approval. While pending, the app shows a calm waiting message.

Refunds

If you'd like a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and request one from Apple. Refund decisions are Apple's alone.

🔒 Opening the grown-up area (parental gate)

Settings and controls live behind a device authentication gate so young children can't wander in. The grown-up area is protected by your device's own security.

  1. Tap to open the grown-up area.
  2. You'll be asked to confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.
  3. If authentication succeeds, you can access the settings. If it fails or is cancelled, the app shows "We couldn't confirm a grown-up. Please try again." and you can try again.

Important: If your device has no passcode set, the app will ask you to set one (Settings › Face ID & Passcode, or Touch ID & Passcode) and there is deliberately no other way in, so a child can't get past it.

Tip: if your child knows your device passcode (for example, if it's their own iPad), the grown-up area is not protected from them. Use a passcode only you know, and consider Apple's Screen Time / Family Sharing controls for additional protection.

Once you pass the gate, you can optionally Sign in with Apple to save your settings on the device, or tap "Continue for now (local)" to set things up without signing in. Either way, nothing is uploaded.

🎂 Changing the age band

  1. Open the grown-up area (confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode — see above).
  2. Find the age band setting.
  3. Choose the band that fits your child. This tunes the experience to your child's level.

⏰ Setting the daily time limit

  1. Open the grown-up area (confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode).
  2. Find the daily time limit setting.
  3. Choose how much time per day your child can spend in the app.

👀 Turning off the activity view

The optional activity view records general topics only (like "space" or "animals") — never your child's exact words, and never anything unsafe. To turn it off:

  1. Open the grown-up area (confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode).
  2. Find the activity setting.
  3. Switch it off. No topics are recorded while it is off.

👋 Removing the parent identity

If you signed in with Apple and want to remove that identity:

  1. Open the grown-up area (confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode).
  2. Choose remove parent / sign out.
  3. The stored Apple identifier and optional name are deleted from the device.

🗑️ Deleting all data

  1. Open the grown-up area (confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode).
  2. Choose delete all data.
  3. This erases the on-device settings, consent record, activity view, Stars, and any parent identity. Deletion is immediate and permanent on the device.

You can also delete everything by removing the app from your device.

❓ FAQ

Does my child need an account?
No. Your child never signs in and is never asked for a name, email, birthday, or any personal detail.

Does it use the internet?
The app itself works fully offline — there is no server, no cloud, and nothing about your child is uploaded. The only internet use is when a grown-up buys or restores a purchase from Apple's App Store.

Where do answers come from?
Answers are made on your device by on-device Apple Intelligence. They are never sent anywhere.

Is it safe? What if my child asks something inappropriate?
Every question is checked by a built-in on-device safety system before and after an answer is made. Unsafe questions are gently redirected, never answered. Sensitive moments (like feelings or asking for help) are met with warm, caring words and trusted helpline information.

Are there ads?
No. There are no ads, no tracking, and no third-party companies.

What data do you collect about my child?
None. Our App Store privacy label is honestly "Data Not Collected." Purchases are handled entirely by Apple — we never see payment details or personal data.

Does it cost anything?
Curio Cove is free to download. The first 3 friends are free forever. A one-time in-app purchase ($9.99 USD) unlocks all 10 friends. No subscription, no ads, no surprises.
