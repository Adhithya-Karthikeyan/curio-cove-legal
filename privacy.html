Curio Cove is a learning app for curious kids (target ages 8–10). This policy explains, in plain English, what happens to information when your child uses the app. The short version: nothing your child does leaves the device, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

🌟 The short version

  • Everything runs on your device. Answers are generated on-device by Apple Intelligence. There is no server, no internet connection, no API keys, and no cloud.
  • Nothing is transmitted. Your child's questions and the app's answers are never uploaded, never stored off the device, and never seen by us or anyone else.
  • No data is collected. Our App Store privacy label is honestly "Data Not Collected."
  • Your child has no account and is never asked for a name, email, birthday, photo, location, or any other personal detail.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party companies.
  • You are in control. A parent can turn off the optional activity view, remove the parent identity, and delete everything with one tap — immediately and permanently, on the device.

👨‍👩‍👧 Who this policy is for

Curio Cove is used by children, but this policy is written for the parents and guardians who set up and manage the app. Children are never asked to read or agree to anything.

🧐 What we collect

We do not collect any personal information — from children or from parents.

Curio Cove has no server and makes no network connections. Because nothing is ever sent off the device, there is no data for us to receive, see, store, sell, or share. We do not use analytics, we do not use advertising, and we do not include any third-party software development kits (SDKs) that could collect data.

🤖 How answers are made (on-device Apple Intelligence)

When your child asks a question, the answer is created locally on the device using Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device Foundation Models technology). The question and the answer stay on the device. They are not sent to us, to Apple as a personal record, or to any other company.

The app works fully offline. No internet connection is required or used to answer questions.

🧒 Children's use — no accounts, no personal information

  • Your child never creates an account and never signs in.
  • Your child is never asked for a name, email address, phone number, birthday, photo, contacts, or location.
  • There is no messaging, no chat with other people, no social features, and no way for your child to reach or be reached by anyone through the app.

🔑 Optional parent sign-in (Sign in with Apple)

Signing in is optional and is only ever done by a grown-up, from inside the parent area (which is protected by a parental gate — see below). If a parent chooses to use Sign in with Apple:

  • Curio Cove requests only a stable Apple identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name. We never request your email address.
  • Hide My Email is fully supported.
  • The identifier and optional name are stored only on the device (in local settings). They are never transmitted anywhere.

If a parent prefers not to sign in, a clearly labeled "Continue for now (local)" option lets them set up the parent area without any sign-in at all.

📱 What is stored on the device (and stays there)

For the app to work and remember your preferences, a small amount of information is saved locally on the device (using the system's standard on-device storage). None of it ever leaves the device. This includes:

  • Guardian settings — such as the chosen age band and the daily time limit.
  • A consent record — a simple note that a grown-up completed setup.
  • An optional, redacted activity view — if you leave it on, it records general topics only (for example, "space" or "animals"). It never records your child's exact words, and it never records anything unsafe or distressing.
  • Your child's Stars — the little rewards collected while learning.
  • Optional parent identity — the Apple identifier and optional name, only if a parent signed in with Apple (see above).

Because this information lives only on the device, it is removed if you delete the app, and you can also erase it at any time from inside the app (see the next section).

🎛️ Your controls as a parent

Inside the grown-up area (reached by solving a simple math parental gate), you can:

  • Turn the activity view off at any time.
  • Remove the parent identity (sign out and delete the stored identifier and name).
  • Delete all data with one tap — this erases the on-device settings, consent record, activity view, Stars, and any parent identity. Deletion is immediate and permanent on the device.

You can also remove everything by deleting the app from your device.

🚫 No advertising, tracking, or third parties

Curio Cove contains no advertisements, no tracking technologies, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing about your child is profiled, targeted, or shared, because nothing is collected in the first place.

🛡️ Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Curio Cove is designed to comply with children's privacy laws, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR provisions for children ("GDPR-K").

We do not collect, use, or disclose any personal information from children. The app has no accounts for children, requests no personal details, and transmits nothing off the device. Any information used by the app (see "What is stored on the device") stays on the device under the parent's control and can be deleted at any time.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions about your child's privacy, please contact us using the email below.

✏️ Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Because the app collects no data and makes no network connections, changes are expected to be rare.
